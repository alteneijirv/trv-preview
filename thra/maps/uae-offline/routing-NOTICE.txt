This independently generated database is separate from proprietary TRV app
code. No Organic Maps source or MWM database is included in these files.
The complete derived graph is provided in routing-graph.bin; the reproducible
data-processing method is provided in routing-builder.py. Input is the frozen
official Geofabrik GCC extract listed with size, date and hash in the manifest.
No contributor username, userID or changeset metadata is included.

Mapped roads/tracks are not a guarantee of current legal access, clearance or
terrain passability. Static route-time estimates are not live traffic. No route
across unmarked dunes is invented when the mapped network is disconnected.
